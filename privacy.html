Privacy Policy

Auto-Video-Upload only accesses information explicitly authorized by the user through TikTok Login Kit.

Authentication is performed using TikTok's official OAuth process.

The application only accesses the permissions granted by the user and uses them exclusively to upload and publish content to the user's own authorized TikTok account.

No personal information is sold, shared, or distributed to third parties.

The application does not collect information beyond what is required to provide its functionality.
